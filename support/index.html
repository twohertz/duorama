Duorama: Support

A vintage film camera for iPhone Duo.

Getting started

Half-fold your iPhone Duo and hold it at your waist, flat half facing up. The half with the cameras stands up and faces your subject; the flat half is the finder you look down into. Press the shutter release beside the glass to take a photo, then turn the crank one full turn to wind on to the next frame. Closed, or opened out flat, the same camera works at eye level.

Tap the glass to raise the magnifier and focus. Slide the film back sideways to change film. Tap the nameplate on the film back for Settings.

Questions

Why aren’t my photos in Photos?

Until you buy, the photos you take wait in the camera, undeveloped: your first roll on each film back. Tap Develop this roll on the contact sheet, or Develop film in Settings. After buying, every waiting photo goes to Photos, then each roll’s contact sheet, and from then on each photo goes to Photos as you take it.

I bought it on another iPhone, or reinstalled the app.

The purchase usually comes back by itself. If it doesn’t, tap Restore Purchases on the contact sheet or in Settings. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The finder shows the picture back to front.

Like a real waist-level finder, the glass shows the picture reversed left to right. Your photos are never reversed. Settings, Finder, Reverse the picture turns it off.

The shutter won’t fire.

After each shot the signal is red and the finder goes dark until you wind on: turn the crank one full turn. At the end of a roll, the contact sheet opens; load a new roll from there (after developing, if you haven’t bought yet).

It says the app needs iPhone Duo.

The waist-level finder needs the fold of iPhone Duo, so the camera only works there. On other iPhone models the app shows that note and nothing else, and offers no purchase.

No shutter sound?

The camera’s sounds follow the Ring/Silent switch. Where the law requires the system’s own shutter sound, that one plays instead.

The camera or Photos isn’t allowed.

Open the iPhone’s Settings app, find Duorama, and allow Camera, and Photos (Add Photos Only). Photos waiting to be saved are kept safe in the meantime.
